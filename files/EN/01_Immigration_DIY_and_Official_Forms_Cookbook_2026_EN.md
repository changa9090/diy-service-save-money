## 2. Four Golden Rules for Error-Free ImmiAccount Lodgement

1. **Cross-Check Every Prior Visa Application First**:
   - The Department of Home Affairs retains every previous employment date, address, and relationship history ever declared on prior Student, WHV, or Visitor forms (retrievable free via FOI if needed). Ensure zero unexplained discrepancies before lodging.
2. **Colour Scans & Certified Translations**:
   - Upload high-resolution colour PDF scans (300 DPI) of original documents. Every non-English civil record must be accompanied by an accredited NAATI translation or an official English-language government extract.
3. **Correct Police Check Codes**:
   - Australian Federal Police (AFP) National Police Checks for migration must specify Purpose Code 33 (Immigration/Citizenship, Complete Disclosure). Standard employment checks are rejected.
4. **Bridging Visa Mechanics**:
   - Lodging a valid onshore substantive visa application while holding a current substantive visa generally grants an associated Bridging Visa A (010), which only comes into effect after your current substantive visa expires.

## 3. Box-by-Box Guide to Key Department of Home Affairs Official Forms

| Official Form | Purpose | Exact Completion Protocol & 2026 Reforms | DIY Mistake to Prevent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Form 80**<br>*Personal particulars for assessment including character assessment* | Detailed character, travel, address & employment vetting | • Complete 10 continuous years (or since age 16) of residential addresses, international travel, and employment/study.<br>• Explicitly list every period of unemployment, gap travel, or study break so there is zero chronological gap (MM/YYYY to MM/YYYY). Use Part T for overflow. | Omitting casual jobs or 2-month unemployment periods triggers s 56 requests or PIC 4020 concealment concerns. |
| **Form 888**<br>*Supporting statement in relation to a Partner or Prospective Marriage visa* | Third-party witness evidence for 820/801, 309/100, 300 | • Key Reform: Form 888 is no longer a Statutory Declaration. Your witness does not need a Justice of the Peace (JP) or lawyer to witness their signature.<br>• Witness writes firsthand observations of financial, household, social, and commitment aspects and signs directly. | Even though JP witnessing is abolished, the form is rejected unless accompanied by the witness's photo ID and proof of Australian citizenship or PR (Australian passport, birth certificate, citizenship certificate, or VEVO). |
| **Form 1229**<br>*Consent form to grant an Australian visa to a child under 18* | Non-migrating parent consent for minor visa applicants | • Must be signed by every person holding legal parental responsibility who is not migrating with the child.<br>• Attach government-issued photo ID bearing the non-migrating parent's signature (or sole-parental-responsibility Court Orders). | Attaching an unsigned ID card or mismatched signature triggers departmental verification delays (s 56). |
| **Form 956A vs Form 956** | Authorised recipient vs Legal representative | • Use Form 956A if nominating a family member or friend solely to receive Departmental correspondence.<br>• Form 956 is strictly reserved for Australian Legal Practitioners and Registered Migration Agents (MARA). | Unregistered third parties giving immigration assistance commit a criminal offence under Migration Act s 280. |
| **Form 1022 vs Form 1023** | Post-lodgement change vs Correcting prior error | • Form 1022 (s 104): Notify new events occurring after lodgement (marriage, separation, birth of child, new passport, new address).<br>• Form 1023 (s 105): Proactively correct an incorrect answer previously given. | Lodge Form 1023 before a visa decision is made to mitigate s 109 cancellation exposure. |
| **Form 1447**<br>*No Further Stay waiver request* | Request to waive Condition 8503 | • Must prove compelling and compassionate circumstances that developed since the visa was granted and were beyond your control, causing a major change (marriage alone is legally insufficient under policy). | Strict Trap: Lodging Form 1447 does not grant a Bridging Visa. Apply at least 4 weeks before your current visa expires. |
