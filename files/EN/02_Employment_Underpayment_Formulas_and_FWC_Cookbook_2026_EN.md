## 2. Step 1: Identify Your Exact Award Classification & Financial-Year Pay Guides

The most common DIY calculation error is multiplying hours worked in 2023, 2024, or 2025 by today's post-1 July 2026 hourly rate. Australian minimum wages and Modern Award rates change every year from the first full pay period on or after 1 July.

### 2.1 How to Look Up Official Historical & Current Rates on FWO

1. Open the Fair Work Ombudsman Pay and Conditions Tool ([calculate.fairwork.gov.au](https://calculate.fairwork.gov.au)).
2. Confirm your exact Modern Award and Classification Level based on actual duties performed (not whatever job title your employer used).
3. Download the PDF Pay Guide from [fairwork.gov.au/pay-and-wages/minimum-wages/pay-guides](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/pay-and-wages/minimum-wages/pay-guides) for each separate Financial Year in which you worked (FY 2022–23, FY 2023–24, FY 2024–25, FY 2025–26, and FY 2026–27).

## 3. Step 2: Exact Mathematical Self-Calculation Formulas

Perform wage reconciliations in Gross (before-tax) dollars. If you were paid undeclared cash-in-hand with zero PAYG tax remitted to the ATO on your MyGov Income Statement, credit the cash received against your Gross statutory entitlement and note that no PAYG tax was withheld.

### Formula 1. Weekly / Fortnightly Shift & Penalty Rate Shortfall

Break every pay period into shift buckets (Ordinary Weekday, Saturday, Sunday, Public Holiday, Overtime First 2h, Overtime >2h, Late-Night/Split-Shift Allowances):

- **Statutory Gross Entitlement**: SUM(Actual Hours Worked in Bucket × Applicable FY Award Rate for Bucket) + Applicable Award Allowances
- **Pay-Period Gross Wage Shortfall**: MAX(0, Statutory Gross Entitlement − Actual Gross Wages Paid)

> **Critical Calculation Rule: Never Compound Casual Weekend Penalty Rates**
> Do not multiply the Casual Ordinary Rate ($33.85) by 1.25 for Saturday ($33.85 × 1.25 = $42.31, which is incorrect).
> Under standard Modern Awards, the 25% casual loading and weekend penalty add together against the Permanent Base Rate (B = $27.08):
> - **Correct Casual Saturday**: $27.08 × 150% = $40.62/hr
> - **Correct Casual Sunday**: $27.08 × 150% = $40.62/hr ([MA000119] Intro–L2) or $27.08 × 175% = $47.39/hr ([MA000009] Hotel/Pub L2 & [MA000119] L3–L6)

### Formula 2. Flat-Rate or Annualised Salary Reconciliation

Even if your contract specifies a flat hourly rate (e.g. "$32/hr all-in") or fixed annual salary (e.g. "$65,000/yr"), an employer cannot legally pay less than the total Modern Award safety net across actual hours, weekends, public holidays, and overtime worked:

- **Salary / Flat-Rate Underpayment**: Total Statutory Award Entitlement Across All Actual Shifts − Total Gross Salary Actually Paid
- *(Note: Without an express contractual set-off clause, above-Award weekday payments cannot automatically offset unpaid weekend penalty obligations.)*

### Formula 3. Accrued Untaken Annual Leave + 17.5% Leave Loading on Termination (NES ss 87, 90(2))

Permanent full-time and part-time employees accrue 4 weeks of paid annual leave per year (4 ÷ 52 = 0.076923 hours per ordinary hour worked, or 152 hours/year on a 38-hour week). Under Fair Work Act s 90(2), untaken annual leave on termination must be paid at the rate payable had the employee taken the leave, including 17.5% Annual Leave Loading where prescribed by the Award:

- **Net Untaken Annual Leave Hours**: (Total Ordinary Hours Worked × 4 ÷ 52) − Paid Annual Leave Hours Taken
- **Unpaid Annual Leave Payout**: Net Untaken Leave Hours × Final Permanent Base Rate (B) × 1.175

*(Note: Untaken Personal/Carer's (sick) leave does not pay out on termination unless an Enterprise Agreement expressly provides for it.)*

### Formula 4. Payment in Lieu of Notice (NES s 117)

If dismissed without receiving full working notice (excluding casuals and genuine serious misconduct):

- **Minimum Notice Scale (s 117(3)(a))**: Service ≤ 1 yr: 1 week · > 1 to ≤ 3 yrs: 2 weeks · > 3 to ≤ 5 yrs: 3 weeks · > 5 yrs: 4 weeks
- **Over-45 Addition (s 117(3)(b))**: Add 1 extra week if over 45 years old (46+) with at least 2 years continuous service (maximum 5 weeks).
- **Notice Shortfall**: (Statutory Notice Weeks − Working Notice Weeks Given) × Full Weekly Rate of Pay (s 18).

### Formula 5. NES Redundancy Pay (NES s 119, Employers with 15+ Employees Only)

Applies to permanent employees (≥12 months service) whose job is abolished by a non-small-business employer (15 or more employees across associated entities), calculated at ordinary-hours Base Rate of Pay (s 16):

- 1–<2 yrs: 4 wks · 2–<3 yrs: 6 wks · 3–<4 yrs: 7 wks · 4–<5 yrs: 8 wks · 5–<6 yrs: 10 wks · 6–<7 yrs: 11 wks · 7–<8 yrs: 13 wks · 8–<9 yrs: 14 wks · 9–<10 yrs: 16 wks · 10+ yrs: 12 wks.

### Formula 6. Unpaid Superannuation Guarantee (12.0% & 1 July 2026 Payday Super, NES s 116B)

- **Historical & Current SG Rates**: FY22–23 = 10.5% · FY23–24 = 11.0% · FY24–25 = 11.5% · From 1 July 2025 (FY25–26 & FY26–27): 12.0%.
- **Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE, ATO SGR 2009/2)**: Includes ordinary hours, casual loading, weekend/shift penalties, and paid leave taken; excludes true overtime hours and termination lump-sum annual leave payouts.
- **1 July 2026 Payday Super Reform & NES s 116B Court Recovery**:
  - From 1 July 2026, employers must pay 12.0% superannuation with every pay cycle so funds arrive in your super fund within 7 business days of payday.
  - Under NES s 116B, workers can recover unpaid superannuation directly inside an FCFCOA s 548 Small Claim (within the $100,000 cap) in addition to reporting to the ATO ([ato.gov.au](https://www.ato.gov.au)).
- **Unpaid Super Shortfall**: (Statutory OTE × Applicable SG% (12.0%)) − Super Contributions Actually Received by Your Fund.

## 5. Missing Payslips? Use s 557C Reverse Onus of Proof & The 4-Tier Evidence Checklist

### 5.1 Payslip & Record-Keeping Breaches (Fair Work Act ss 535–536, reg 3.46) + s 557C Reverse Onus

Employers must keep accurate time and wages records for 7 years (s 535), issue compliant written or electronic payslips within 1 working day of payday (s 536(1)), and never issue knowingly false or misleading payslips (s 536(3)). Under Fair Work Regulations reg 3.46, every payslip must state the employer's legal name and ABN, pay period, payment date, gross and net pay, hourly rate and hours worked, loadings/penalties, deductions, and 12% superannuation amount plus fund name.

- **3-Step DIY Action Protocol for Payslip Breaches**:
  1. Send a short written request (citing ss 535–536) asking for all missing payslips and time/wages records within 7 days.
  2. Invoke s 557C Reverse Onus of Proof: If the employer failed to keep records or issue payslips without reasonable excuse, the legal burden of proof shifts onto the employer in Court to disprove your reasonable shift reconstruction.
  3. Lodge a parallel regulatory breach report at [fairwork.gov.au](https://www.fairwork.gov.au).

### 5.2 Mandatory 4-Tier Evidence Checklist

1. **Tier 1 (Exact Corporate Entity & s 550 Accessorial Proof)**: Employment contract, job ad, and [abr.business.gov.au](https://abr.business.gov.au) ABN/ACN search confirming the exact Pty Ltd legal name (do not sue a shopfront trading name alone), plus texts/emails showing which individual director controlled payroll (s 550).
2. **Tier 2 (Payment & Tax/Super Trail)**: Highlighted bank statements, any payslips received, MyGov → ATO STP Income Statements, and Super Fund transaction statements.
3. **Tier 3 (Contemporaneous Hours Reconstruction for s 557C)**: Roster photos, Deputy / Tanda / Square app screenshots (export before losing login access), SMS/WhatsApp shift messages, Google Maps Timeline location exports, public transport card logs, and timestamped opening/closing photos.
4. **Tier 4 (Self-Calculated Schedule)**: Completed print-out of Park_Co_Underpayment_Self_Calculator_2026_EN.xlsx.

## 6. Step-by-Step Recovery & Official Form Completion Guide (FCFCOA & FWC)

### Step 1. Serve a Formal 7-Day Written Letter of Demand

Email your Schedule of Underpayment to the employer (Pty Ltd + Director), citing the exact Modern Award classification, s 536 / s 557C (if payslips were missing), and requesting payment of Gross wages and Super within 7 days, failing which you will file in the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (s 548).

- **Exact Forms Required** ([fcfcoa.gov.au](https://www.fcfcoa.gov.au) via eLodgment):
  1. Originating Application - Fair Work Division
  2. Form 5 - Small claim under the Fair Work Act 2009 *(Trap: Do not use Form 2; Form 2 is exclusively for General Protections Dismissal claims)*
- **Verified Filing Fees (from 1 July 2026, recoverable from employer upon success)**:
  - Claim under $10,000: $310
  - Claim $10,000 to $50,000: $500

- **Key Box-by-Box Tips**: Name the exact Pty Ltd + ACN as Respondent; attach your completed Excel Schedule; claim s 547 pre-judgment interest plus filing-fee reimbursement; serve stamped copies on the ASIC registered office and file an Affidavit of Service.
