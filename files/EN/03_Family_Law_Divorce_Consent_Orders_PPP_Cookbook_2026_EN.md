## 1. Why Fault-Based Breakup Battles Waste Money: Australia's 3 Family Law Pillars & When to Instruct Park & Co

Callers going through a painful separation sometimes ask lawyers to *"prove my ex-partner's infidelity or bad behaviour in court."* Under the *Family Law Act 1975* (Cth), fault-finding achieves nothing legally and rapidly burns the family asset pool in legal fees. Understanding Australia's three core statutory pillars saves you thousands of dollars:

1. **Pillar 1: Strict No-Fault Divorce (Family Law Act s 48)**:
   - Australia has zero fault-based divorce or moral damages. The Court asks only one question: has the marriage broken down irretrievably, proved by 12 months + 1 clear day of separation?
   - Divorce Does Not Divide Property or Parenting: Once a Divorce Order takes final effect (1 month + 1 day after grant), a strict 12-month clock (s 44(3)) starts ticking to file property/maintenance orders in the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (FCFCOA), or 2 years from separation for de facto couples under s 44(5).
2. **Pillar 2: The 4-Step Property Settlement Framework (ss 79 / 90SM + 10 June 2025 Reforms)**:
   - Courts divide assets strictly across four objective steps: (1) identify and value the net global asset, liability, and superannuation pool (both Australian and overseas assets under mandatory Full & Frank Financial Disclosure); (2) assess financial, non-financial, and homemaker/parenting contributions (from 10 June 2025, economic or financial abuse is expressly codified as a statutory factor, alongside specific rules for companion animals / family pets); (3) adjust for future needs (age, health, income disparity, care of children); and (4) ensure the final split is Just and Equitable.
3. **Pillar 3: Parenting (s 60CC Best Interests & Mandatory s 60I Mediation)**:
   - Following the 2024–2025 reforms, there is no presumption of equal shared parental responsibility; the Court focuses strictly on six core Best Interests & Safety of the Child factors. Unless urgent family violence or child abuse applies, parents must attempt Family Dispute Resolution (Family Relationship Centre 1800 050 321) and obtain a s 60I Certificate before filing in Court.

## 2. DIY Online Divorce (comcourts.gov.au): Incorporating the 10 June 2025 Reforms & 1 July 2026 Fees

1. **Core Eligibility**: At least 12 months + 1 clear day of permanent separation (e.g. separated 1 May 2025 → earliest filing date 2 May 2026), plus Australian citizenship, permanent residency, or 12 months ordinary residence.
2. **Major 10 June 2025 Reforms** (*Family Law Amendment Act 2024*):
   - Short-Marriage Counselling Repealed: Couples married for less than 2 years are no longer required to attend counselling or file a counselling certificate.
   - Court Attendance Abolished for Most Sole Applicants: Sole applicants with children under 18 no longer need to attend a court hearing unless the respondent files an objection or substituted/dispensed service is sought.
3. **Verified Filing Fees (Effective 1 July 2026)**:
   - Full Divorce Filing Fee: $1,170
   - Reduced Fee (eligible concession card / financial hardship): $390
   - Joint Application Trap: On a Joint Application, both spouses must hold eligible concession cards to receive the $390 reduced fee; if only one spouse holds a card, the full $1,170 fee applies.
4. **Separation Under One Roof & Sole Service Rules**:
   - Separation Under One Roof: Requires an Affidavit explaining separate sleeping, finances, domestic duties, and public notification (Sole = Applicant Affidavit + 1 independent adult witness Affidavit; Joint = separate Affidavits from both spouses).
   - Sole Service (Divorce Service Kit): Never hand divorce papers to your spouse yourself. An adult third party (18+) or process server must serve the respondent at least 28 days before hearing (within Australia) or 42 days (overseas), completing Acknowledgment of Service, Affidavit of Service, and Affidavit Proving Signature.

## 3. Consent Orders ($215) & Priority Property Pools (PPP <= $550,000)

1. **Application for Consent Orders ($215 filing fee)**:
   - Informal written agreements have zero binding finality under the *Family Law Act* and risk later challenges (or setting aside under s 79A if assets are concealed).
   - Mandatory Superannuation Split Rule: You must serve the proposed Minute on the Superannuation Fund Trustee at least 28 days before filing to obtain written procedural fairness confirmation.
   - Identical .docx Rule: Upload both the signed PDF Minute and a word-for-word identical editable .docx Minute, plus a Notice of Child Abuse, Family Violence or Risk if parenting orders are sought.
2. **Contested Small Pools: Priority Property Pools (PPP <= $550,000)**:
   - Where net property excluding superannuation is $550,000 or less, use the streamlined PPP Financial Summary alongside an Initiating Application (1 July 2026 fees: Financial or Parenting Final = $455 / $610 with Interim; Both = $740 / $895 with Interim).
   - Parenting Pre-Action (s 60I Certificate) & Page Limits: Unless urgent family violence or child abuse applies, obtain an FDR s 60I Certificate first (e.g. via Family Relationship Centre 1800 050 321). Interim affidavits are capped at 10 pages.
